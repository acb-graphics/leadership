Carnival Cruise Line is an international cruise line headquartered in Doral, Florida. Their iOS and Android app features a pre-onboarding and on-cruise experience. The app had been flagged for not complying with ADA standards and needed a heuristic evaluation and accessibility recommendations.

01

Challenge

The team inherited a legacy app designed and engineered by another agency.

We iterated closely with the client's design team to figure out gaps in the user's intended environment. The challenge was to audit designs and create solutions that improved accessibility and experience for guests onboard the ship. The testing environment had to be engineered to mimic an onboard experience, and every recommendation had to meet ADA AA standards for the new release.

Why it was hard

  • Inherited a legacy app built by another agency — audits had to work around existing architecture.
  • Every recommendation had to pass ADA AA standards, not just look better.
  • The onboard testing environment — including ship connectivity — had to be engineered to feel real.

Strategy

Accessibility evaluation can be split into development and visual improvements.

I worked with a developer to build a spreadsheet of changes ranging from screen-reader hierarchy to color contrast and button sizing. We then mapped where each element applied across the app in different scenarios — new user, returning user, pre-board, onboard, and error states such as internet connectivity issues.

How the audit was scoped

  • Split into two tracks: development changes (screen-reader hierarchy) and visual changes (contrast, sizing).
  • Every recommendation logged in a shared spreadsheet with the dev team.
  • Applied against five real scenarios: new user, returning user, pre-board, onboard, and connectivity errors.

03

Approach

The Carnival team needed a design system to create an AA-compliant, accessible app.

Working closely with the product manager, we ran these iterations in sprints and made improvements according to frequency of usage and feedback from testing onboard the ship. We could brainstorm further on key experiences, like pre-boarding, to keep the user's retention and excitement in the Carnival Cruise app experience. The result was a compliant app that passed ADA's assessment.

What shipped

  • A documented design system built specifically for AA compliance.
  • Sprint-based iteration prioritized by usage frequency and real onboard feedback.
  • The result: a compliant app that passed Carnival's ADA assessment.
