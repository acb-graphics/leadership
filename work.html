Strategy, systems and business outcome
The four that best show scope of influence, and where design changed the direction rather than the artifact.
Home→

An 8-week 0-to-1 engagement taking a client from a vague idea to a validated product direction, brand and roadmap.

Outcome — the client used the roadmap to hire a permanent design and engineering team. 410 research responses; full competitive landscape map presented to investors.

Product Researcher & Designer · Renga · Product strategy
Deciem→

Five departments each working from a different picture of the customer. The design problem turned out to be an organizational one.

Outcome — five departments aligned on one prioritized view of customer need. Two Spark AR concepts developed — Virtual Foundation Matching and a Terracycle recycling filter — extending engagement beyond physical retail.

Senior Experience Consultant & Researcher · Deciem · Organizational research
Equinox+→

One design system carried across Roku, Android TV, Fire TV and Apple TV — designed to the tightest platform's file-size ceiling rather than cut back to it.

Outcome — MVP shipped on four platforms and the system handed off to Equinox's in-house design team. Renga's own designers came out of it able to work in 10-foot.

VP of Design // Product Design Lead · Renga · Design systems
NBC Universal→

A unified TV experience holding every NBC brand under one roof — architected to scale for channels that didn't exist yet.

Outcome — the concept went back for stakeholder review, and the direction that emerged became Peacock TV. Six top-level areas unified under one discoverability layer.

Lead Product Designer · TribalScale · Platform strategy
CBS Sports HQ→

A 24/7 live channel with a drop-off problem: too much content, too much cognitive load. Picture-in-picture kept the broadcast live while browsing.

Outcome — shipped across Apple TV, Roku and Fire TV, then refined from real early-adopter behaviour. Home page moved from full-screen to collapsed video to restore user control.

Lead Product Designer · TribalScale · OTT / Connected TV
Craft under constraint

Carnival Cruise Line→

An accessibility audit and remediation of an inherited legacy app, rebuilt to a documented AA conformance standard.

Outcome — the app passed its accessibility assessment at AA conformance, having previously not met requirements — with a design system built to hold it there.

Lead Product Designer · TribalScale · Accessibility
1800Flowers Alexa→

Designing with no screen, against an Amazon Pay integration that wasn't publicly documented yet.

Outcome — shipped an end-to-end voice purchase flow closing the loop with Amazon Pay. Invented a testing method — unplugged device, facilitator voicing Alexa over Zoom — to research a product that didn't exist.

Lead Product Designer · Accedo · Voice / Conversational
Honda × Parkopedia→

Honda's in-house team had mapped the happy path. I mapped everything they hadn't — error states, and the experience of searching while the car is moving.

Outcome — Honda stakeholders approved the concept and the Parkopedia partnership, and it became the design roadmap Honda and Parkopedia continued building on.

Lead Product Designer · Honda R&D · In-vehicle UX
