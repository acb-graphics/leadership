My journey began with graphic design and expanded into designing for physical spaces before pixels. Across startups and agencies, I’ve had the opportunity to help ship products spanning voice, TV, automotive, and wearables. Along the way, I became especially interested in the systems and strategies surrounding the work, not just the visual details. Today, I lead the Dish and Sling TV growth design teams, helping steer strategy, set direction, and empower subject matter experts to execute with clarity and care.

02

How I lead

Listen, be kind, stay curious and choose diversity over monoculture.

My role as a leader is to clear the road, not to occupy it. I lead by caring personally and challenging directly — working and advocating for my team, and building a space where everyone at the table is invited as a co-creator.

01

Advocacy builds partnership

I prefer working with cross functional pods with clear decision making authority, and I trust the subject matter experts I hire to lead where they have expertise. To me, design advocacy and inviting co-creators into the process is how trust is built and strong internal partnerships take shape.

02

Everyone at the table is a designer

Engineers, PMs, copywriters, and customer service teams all contribute to the work in meaningful ways. Some of the strongest insights have come from people whose title wasn’t “designer”, and I’ve learned that the best outcomes come from humility, curiosity, and shared ownership.

03

Experimenting towards direction

I don’t believe in waiting for the perfect direction. Instead, I facilitate conversations to clarify goals and intent. I experiment and share early and frequently to motivate and propel things forward.

03

What I specialize in

My specialty is building design teams — DesignOps leadership that bridges discovery and strategy.

That covers hiring and onboarding, improving operational efficiency as a company scales, and managing design production across digital and physical omnichannels — from discovery and research through to strategy led execution.

I'm passionate about finding new methods to create thoughtful, user-centric experiences that meet both customer and business needs. Investment in research and testing is what surfaces areas of opportunity, but experiential design is what builds retention and draws new customers in.

Areas of practice

  • DesignOps and team structure — hiring, onboarding, scaling process as the org grows.
  • Research validation — facilitator and observer across qualitative and quantitative studies.
  • Design systems and growth funnels — acquisition and retention.
  • IoT experience design — TV, console, and connected devices.
  • Design facilitation — using foresight and co-creation methodologies to find new verticals.

05

Education

Currently pursuing a MDes in Strategic Foresight and Innovation at OCAD University

Why am I pursuing a Masters? I kept hitting the limits of what user research can tell us. Research captures the present well, but it doesn’t always reveal where behavior is heading next based on trends and macro factors. I use foresight methods like causal layered analysis and Three Horizons to look beyond litany, understand deeper worldviews, and frame a path from the current system to a preferred future.

Sample research study: The Systems of Information Dissemination, a systems study of the causes of disinformation that was presented at Systems Thinking Ontario.
