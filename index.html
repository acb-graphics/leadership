Designing systems

Range compounds into pattern recognition. Mentored a design team of twelve while leading client strategy, and started solving structural problems — accessibility standards, content architecture, cross-platform consistency.

TribalScale · NBC, CBS Sports HQ, Carnival

Systems of people

The realisation that the hardest system is usually the org. Diagnosed cross-functional misalignment at Deciem, then ran 0-to-1 strategy that a client hired a permanent team from.

Deciem · Renga · Home

Futures and teams

Grew Renga's practice from one designer to nine as VP of Design, alongside an MDes in Strategic Foresight. Now managing two growth teams — using foresight and systems tools to set direction, not just ship features.
