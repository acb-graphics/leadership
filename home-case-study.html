01

Challenge

The client approached our team with an idea for a product but needed to verify market fit.

We also needed to brainstorm what a minimal viable product would actually look like. The Renga team and I built out an 8-week plan covering product strategy, research, positioning, and concept testing.

Why it was hard

  • No existing product — market fit had to be validated before anything got built.
  • Needed a real MVP definition, not just a feature list.
  • All of it — strategy, research, positioning, concept testing — had to fit inside 8 weeks.

02

Strategy

In this phase, we had to build a brand from the ground up and create an investor deck for this startup to gain funding.

We held about four workshops, 1.5 hours each, to identify the product mission, positioning, tone and voice, along with a competitive analysis. We branded Home with beige tones and approachable illustrations to differentiate it from other competitors in the market. The result was an approachable brand that sparked real interest — many people signed up for the email list on joinhome.com.

What got built

  • Four workshops (1.5 hrs each) to nail mission, positioning, tone and voice, plus competitive analysis.
  • A full brand system — beige tones, approachable illustration — to stand apart in a crowded market.
  • A competitive landscape map showing exactly where Home sat across the full home-buying journey.

03

Discovery

Once we understood the product's positioning and had drafted a few concept user flows and wireframes, I ran a quantitative survey.

I reached out to my network and received about 410 responses. The survey asked what stage people were at in buying a home, and their thoughts on the process. Under each stage, I bucketed individuals interested in talking further through video conferencing, for qualitative feedback and concept testing.

Research at a glance

  • 410 responses collected from a quantitative outreach survey.
  • Respondents segmented by where they were in the home-buying journey.
  • Interested respondents bucketed for follow-up video interviews and concept testing.
04

Approach

This is the final shareout with the Home stakeholders.

We tested initial concepts through qualitative interviews and came away with a much better understanding of the buying process at each stage. The client was able to take this prototype and find full-time developers and designers to join Home's team and keep building. Going in, the client understood what they wanted to build — but not necessarily the problem, or what we were trying to solve. This project helped clarify the problem and produced a product roadmap of what's to come.

What shipped

  • A tested prototype, validated through qualitative interviews across each buying stage.
  • Enough clarity for the client to hire full-time developers and designers.
  • A product roadmap translating "what they wanted to build" into the actual problem to solve.
