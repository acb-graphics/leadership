The Honda R&D Innovations team approached us with the challenge of creating a POC on their auto platforms that helps users find and pay for parking. Partnering with Parkopedia, I supported designing user flows and hi-fi mockups for a driver's journey in situ, in motion, and by voice. The concept was presented to Honda stakeholders and now ships as a parking-assist feature in the HondaLink app.

01

Strategy

I started with an analysis of the existing designs from Honda's in-house design team.

There were happy paths already illustrated, but error states and other scenarios — like the experience of searching or paying while the car was in motion — hadn't been considered. I went back to the drawing board to map out edge cases and user states, to get a clearer picture of what still needed to be designed.

What was missing

  • Existing designs mapped happy paths, but not error states.
  • The in-motion experience — searching or paying while driving — hadn't been considered at all.
  • Mapping edge cases and user states first surfaced what still needed design work.

02

Discovery

After presenting the edge cases to the internal team, we built a prototype that distinguishes when a user is in situ, in motion, or parked.

Payment was a hurdle that took real discovery and collaboration with the development team, to understand how CarPlay and Android Auto each handled the security of stored payment methods.

Discovery focus

  • A prototype that distinguishes in situ, in motion, and parked states.
  • Payment security was the biggest unknown — required close collaboration with the dev team.
  • How CarPlay and Android Auto each handle payment-method security shaped the final flow.

03

Approach

We built an interactive prototype in InVision, presented to internal stakeholders to help prioritize features for upcoming sprints.

The development team tested a few scenarios during those sprints, in order to present a working prototype to Honda's stakeholders. They were happy with the result and the partnership with Parkopedia — which led to the launch of this feature on the HondaLink app.

What shipped

  • An interactive InVision prototype used to prioritize features for upcoming sprints.
  • The dev team tested real scenarios during sprints before presenting to Honda's stakeholders.
  • The Parkopedia partnership led to a shipped parking-assist feature in the HondaLink app.
04

Outcome

The result is a parking-assist feature now live in the HondaLink app — letting drivers find, reserve and pay for parking without breaking the flow of driving.
