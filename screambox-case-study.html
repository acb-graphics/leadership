Case Study — OTT / Console Launch

Screambox

A cinematic, horror-first streaming app built for a console that didn't exist yet — designed in lockstep with PS4's own launch.

Screambox is the ultimate destination for horror movie fans — un-cut, commercial-free horror movies and TV programs, launched exclusively alongside PS4.

01

Challenge

Before the launch of PS4, the client wanted to launch an app alongside the release of the new gaming platform.

Working with no guidelines — PS4 was a brand-new system — I worked closely with the engineering team to determine the feasibility of designs for an unreleased system. Without native standards for navigation and controls, we built an experience that felt familiar to other OTT apps: cinematic, immersive, and easy to navigate. That let our client piggyback on the momentum of a console launch and reach new customers among the earliest adopters.

Why it was hard

  • No written development or design constraints existed yet — PS4 was still unreleased.
  • Native navigation and controller standards weren't documented, so feasibility had to be worked out directly with engineering.
  • The app had to feel instantly familiar while still fitting a brand-new console's controls.
Fig. 01 Reconstruction of the early wireframe set — built with no PS4 documentation to reference, so every screen was a blue-sky proposal tested against engineering feasibility.
02

Strategy

Wireframes were built with blue-sky ideas, since PS4 provided no written development or design constraints.

Development worked in tandem with our design team to create a structure and navigation that felt like the new PS4 home experience itself. With that structure in place, the app focused on a cinematic landing experience and enhanced exploration — pulling organized rows of content to enrich browsing across an extensive library.

How structure was set

  • Navigation modeled after the PS4 home experience itself, since no native app guidelines existed.
  • Content organized into rows to make a large library easy to browse visually.
  • Landing experience built to feel cinematic first, functional second — without sacrificing navigability.

03

Approach

The Screambox brand comes through in cinematic backgrounds, red-themed color, and haunting textures — without distracting from the content and browsing experience.

Smooth transitions and app controls native to the PlayStation experience help bring each piece of content forward intuitively.

Brand principles applied

  • Cinematic backgrounds and haunting textures set the tone without competing with content thumbnails.
  • Red used as the single accent color — reserved for focus states and calls to action.
  • Controls and transitions built native to the PlayStation experience, not ported from mobile or web patterns.
