01

How I lead

I lead by caring personally and challenging directly — working for my team rather than the other way around, and building a space where everyone at the table is a designer.

Working for the team doesn't mean waiting for direction. It means setting one clear enough that the team can argue with it — then getting out of the way of the people who know the problem best.

01

Work for the team

The manager's job is to clear the road, not to occupy it. I'd rather be the person removing the blocker than the one approving the work.

02

Pods over hierarchy

Small cross-functional pods with real decision-making authority. Consistent communication matters more than org charts.

03

Everyone at the table is a designer

Engineers, PMs, copywriters, customer service. The best insights in my career have come from people whose job title wasn't "designer."

04

Advocacy builds partnership

Design advocacy isn't self-promotion — it's how internal partnerships get built and how design earns a seat earlier in the process.

Above all: listen, be kind, keep learning, and choose diversity over monoculture.

02

What I specialize in

My specialty is building design teams. Today that means DesignOps leadership that bridges discovery and strategy.

That covers hiring and onboarding, improving operational efficiency as a company scales, and managing design production across digital and physical omnichannel work — from discovery and research through to strategy-led execution.

I'm passionate about finding new methods to create thoughtful, user-centric experiences that meet both customer and business needs. Investment in research and testing is what surfaces the areas of opportunity; experience design is what builds retention and draws new customers in.

Areas of practice

  • DesignOps and team structure — hiring, onboarding, scaling process as the org grows.
  • Research validation — facilitator and observer across qualitative and quantitative studies.
  • Design systems and growth funnels — acquisition and retention.
  • 10-foot experience design — TV, console, and connected-device interfaces.
  • Where I'm heading — co-creation design, and using foresight to find new verticals: moving audiences from media consumption toward active participation.
